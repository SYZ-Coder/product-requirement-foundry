# Proposal: Add Close-Connection Comment Priority

## Change Summary

Add a close-connection ordering boost to the feed detail comment list while preserving pinned comment behavior, moderation visibility, fallback ranking, and tracking compatibility.

## Why

- Familiar voices are easier for users to notice.
- The change improves comment relevance for social products without changing interaction semantics.

## Boundaries

- No public relationship score exposure
- No change to moderation or block rules
- No change to pagination semantics
