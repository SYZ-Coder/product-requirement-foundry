# Social Domain Pack

Use this domain pack for consumer social products, community products, instant messaging hybrids, and content-driven social apps.

## Focus Areas

- social graph
- content publishing
- comment and reply systems
- ranking and recommendation
- message and notification flows
- moderation and governance
- growth and activation loops

## Baseline Modules to Check First

- user profile
- follow/friend relationship
- feed or discovery
- content detail
- comment system
- notification center
- direct message
- moderation and reporting
- tracking and recommendation signals

## Typical Incremental Change Categories

- ranking adjustments
- comment interaction upgrades
- new content entry points
- relationship-based exposure rules
- onboarding and activation enhancements
- message and notification refinements
- moderation policy changes

## Social-Specific Review Questions

- Does the change affect recommendation or exposure logic?
- Does it depend on relationship type such as follow, mutual follow, friend, or block?
- Does it affect moderation, reporting, or anti-abuse flows?
- Does it change creator or viewer incentives?
- Does it require new tracking events for exposure, click, interaction, or conversion?
